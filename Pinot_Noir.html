Pinot Noir

This field is nestled in the bottom of a cool valley with good sun exposure. Previously sold to well known high end producers.

  • Appelation: Russian River
  • Variety: Pinot Noir Clone 115 (80%) and 777 (20%)
  • Rootstock: SO4
  • Spacing: 8 X 5 / 2.4 Acres
  • Trellising: VSP
  • Pruning: Cane
  • Expected Crop: +/- 7 tons
