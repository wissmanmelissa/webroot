Home Page

Welcome to Green Pastures Valley! We have been family owned and operated since 1953 and we've been managing this property for five generations. Originally a prune orchard, we planted our first vineyard in 1973. Our grapes offer a unique flavor as the property spans across both the Russian River and Dry Creek Valley regions. We grow Russian River Chardonnay, Dry Creek Zinfandel, Russian River Pinot Noir, and Dry Creek Sangiovese. We are certified sustainable and fish-friendly.
